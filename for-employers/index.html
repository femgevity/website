Frequently asked by benefits leaders and payer executives
Is FemGevity a separate point solution we'd need to procure?
No. FemGevity is delivered through partnered practices in your existing network — not as a separate vendor relationship for you to underwrite, contract, or administer. Your employees access care through providers they already see, using their existing in-network coverage.
How do we measure outcomes for VBC purposes?
FemGevity tracks outcomes using validated clinical scoring (Menopause Rating Scale), claims-based utilization metrics, and longitudinal biomarker trends. We can provide quarterly outcome reports to participating health plans and self-insured employers for VBC contracting purposes.
Can we expand coverage in our network?
Yes. Payers and large self-insured employers can accelerate FemGevity adoption across their network by supporting practices that join the FemGevity partnership. We provide implementation support and quarterly outcome reporting for network-level VBC programs.
