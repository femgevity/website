Frequently asked by fertility center leaders
How does this differ from referring patients to a menopause specialist?
Outside referrals frequently leak the patient out of your practice. FemGevity operates as an extension of your practice, not a separate destination. Documentation flows back to your EMR. The patient stays under your oversight. The relationship stays yours.
What about clinical decisions on hormone therapy for our patient population?
Our 150+ proprietary protocols are governed by our Medical Advisory of board-certified specialists trained at Duke, Brown, Harvard, and Yale. Every protocol decision is reviewed against validated clinical guidelines, including the Menopause Rating Scale and current cardiovascular and oncology evidence.
Will FemGevity offer fertility services that compete with ours?
No. Our scope is hormonal and longevity care. A non-compete guarantee is included in every partnership contract. We are designed to extend your relationship, never to fragment it.
