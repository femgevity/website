Frequently asked by health system leaders
How does FemGevity integrate with our EMR?
All encounter notes, lab results, prescriptions, and care plans return directly to your physicians' EMR. We currently integrate with Athena and are building integrations with Epic, Cerner, and eClinicalWorks. Your clinicians maintain full visibility and oversight at all times.
What about compliance risk?
FemGevity operates as an MSO-structured entity. Claims flow through your practices' existing billing infrastructure and payer contracts — we never bill payers directly. The compliance posture is no different from any specialist consult inside your network.
How fast can a partnership launch?
Pilot deployments at 1–3 anchor sites typically launch within 60–90 days of contract signature. Full health-system rollout follows within 6–12 months. Founding partners receive priority clinical integration support and dedicated implementation resources.
