Frequently asked by practice owners
How does revenue actually flow?
Every encounter is a CPT-coded visit billed through your existing payer contracts and reimbursed at your existing rates. FemGevity is paid via a separate MSO arrangement — not a percentage of patient billings. Your revenue model on the visit is the same as any other in-network encounter.
Will my patients have to switch insurance or pay out of pocket?
No. Your patients use their existing in-network coverage through your practice. There's no separate cash-pay component, no membership fee on top of insurance, and no out-of-network surprise.
What does my role look like day-to-day?
You refer panel patients into the FemGevity pathway. We handle intake, labs, protocol selection, telehealth visits, and longitudinal follow-up. Every encounter note, lab, and care plan returns to your EMR. You stay the primary provider on the chart.
